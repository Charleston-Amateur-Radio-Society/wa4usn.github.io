The  Armed Forces Day Cross Band Test (formerly known as the MARS cross-band test) will be held on Saturday 10 May 2025. The USS Yorktown NWKJ will be one of the military stations participating.

In this exercise the military stations will be transmitting on military frequencies and monitoring ham frequencies for responses.

Now is the time to learn how to operate “split” on your HF transceiver.

The attached document has all the information.
