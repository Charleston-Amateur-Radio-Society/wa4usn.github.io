SSTV activity will resume around 11:00 UTC and will end on Thursday, October 9, 2025 (time to be determined).  The ARISS Series 29 images of 6 images wants to celebrate the launch of the first artificial Earth satellite in 1957.  This will impact the availability of APRS on 145.825 MHz. The SSTV transmissions will be made using the amateur radio station in the ISS Service Module.The transmission frequency will be 145.800 MHz FM, (+/- 3,5 kHz Doppler shift) using SSTV mode PD120. The ISS callsign will be RS0ISS.
