This annual MARS event is going to happen this year. It will be on 7-8 May 2021.

Unfortunately COVID concerns will preclude the Yorktown’s activation.

You may have operated split before, but has your split included non-ham bands? This exercise lets the Department of Defense see how well the military can operate with civilian stations from their assigned bands.

More information is in the attachment, including times and frequencies.
