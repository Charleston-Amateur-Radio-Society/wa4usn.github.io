MARS operators will supervise the annual cross-band operating day on the Yorktown. Amateur stations should listen on the designated frequencies.  Military stations will announce the\
amateur band frequency they will monitor for calls.  Note:  All times are Zulu (EDT + 4 hours).\
\
CARS members will operate YORKTOWN using it’s Navy call sign, NWKJ.

Anyone interested in assisting should contact Carl Dinge, WD4CXW at (843) 697-4372\
or [1596tcrc@earthlink.com](mailto:1596tcrc@earthlink.com).
