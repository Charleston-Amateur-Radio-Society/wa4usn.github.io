Go to <https://www.fcc.gov/universal-licensing-system> and click on **File Online** (<https://wireless2.fcc.gov/UlsEntry/licManager/login.jsp>)

Login and in the left column click on **My Licenses**

In the Applicant Name and Address box you’ll see a field **E:** with your email address. If there isn’t an email, click on **Update** in the **Work on this License** box on the right. You’ll have to answer the felony question and remember to submit.

Once you submit, it will be a pending action on your license (remember that if you take an upgrade test and they ask that question on the 605 form). It will take a day or two for the FCC to process it and send you an email confirmation.
