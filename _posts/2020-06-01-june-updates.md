There will be a licensing test session on Saturday 13 June at Tall Pines Baptist Church in Ladson. Pre-registration is required, email nr4cb.ve@gmail.com for more information. This will be the last test session before the Extra class question pool changes.

Field day will be held on the Yorktown as usual, assuming patriots point is open. We plan to operate as 2A with GOTA. Expect limited interaction with others, and byob (bring your own bag of food). Email kw4xq@att.net for information. Watch https://tinyurl.com/cars-events for signups.

2021 is the 50th anniversary of CARS. If you would like to help with updating our history and organizing the party, email carsinfo@wa4usn.org.
