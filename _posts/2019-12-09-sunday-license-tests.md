The VE team we work with normally tests on a Saturday in “even” months in Ladson. They have proposed Sunday sessions downtown in “odd” months.

There will be license tests at the VA hospital downtown at 2pm Sunday 19 January 2020 and 15 March 2020. Parking on Sundays is free and plentiful. Access to the hospital is easy from the Crosstown.

Spread the word and let’s see if they’re successful.
