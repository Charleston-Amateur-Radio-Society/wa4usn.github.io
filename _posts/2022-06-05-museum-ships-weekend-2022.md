We activated all four of “our” ships this weekend, and while the bands weren’t cooperative, we made over 100 contacts per ship.

We had a good turnout of operators, loggers, and support, making this a fun event. And being the DX is good practice.

To top it all off, a Patriots Point docent heard us, and brought a family up to see what we did. The kids were pretty interested in what we can do.
