ATTENTION ALL CARS MEMBERS ………..

You and your Guest are cordially invited to attend the

50TH ANNIVERSARY CELEBRATION AND BANQUET of the Charleston Amateur Radio Society aboard the USS Yorktown located at Patriots Point Maritime Museum in Mt. Pleasant, SC.

WHEN:  October 9th, 2021

TIME: 6:00 PM til 10:00 PM

The evening events will include a cocktail hour (cash bar), dinner, recognition of charter members, and Rear Admiral James H. Flatley III as our guest speaker.

Dress for the banquet is dress casual. This is a free event (except for the cash bar) Parking and Admission is free also.  A shuttle service and elevator are available for those who need it.

RSVP is required:    Please contact  Brian Smith, KN4EXJ, at (843) 696-7104 or by email at [bsmith4210@yahoo.com](mailto:bsmith4210@yahoo.com)   Deadline is October 1, 2021
