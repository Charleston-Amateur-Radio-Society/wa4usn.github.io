After decades of CARS occupying Auxiliary Steering on the O2 deck, frame 1, Patriots Point has announced that there is asbestos there that must be mitigated. We are being relocated behind the mast on the starboard side, still on the O2 level. We will need to inventory and move our gear soon, and relocate our HF antenna.

The move is being used an opportunity to update the MOU between the club and Patriots Point. A copy is attached for your reference so you can understand our relationship.
